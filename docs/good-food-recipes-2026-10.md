## Installation

The pack installs on state hydration after the previous packs. It checks recipe IDs, normalized source URLs and titles (including known alternate titles) before adding entries. It does not overwrite existing recipes. A saved installation marker prevents edited or deleted recipes from being reinstalled. All 20 are different from the existing bundled recipes; a household's own matching recipe is skipped.

Servings and quantities use the published recipe data. Where the source gives ranges, the selected value and original range are recorded in notes. Accompaniments without amounts remain unquantified, or are described as planner side suggestions. The macaroni recipe uses the source's vegetarian hard-cheese alternative. Soup recipes are available for lunch and dinner; coronation chicken is a lunch filling.
